Casa Larapa

Cusco · 2023 · Built

A house for a family of five on a terraced site above Cusco, organised around a single courtyard that pulls light and mountain views into the plan.

Two stepped volumes follow the slope and share the courtyard between them. Local stone anchors the lower level; a lighter upper floor opens toward the valley.
