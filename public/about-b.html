About

Architecture and development. Based in New York.

I came to architecture through engineering and real estate development. These backgrounds continue to shape the way I approach design: as an intersection of structure, material, form, economics, and culture.

My work has evolved alongside my background. Engineering taught me to approach problems through systems, mathematics, and technical constraints; real estate development introduced questions of efficiency, economics, and performance; and architecture opened a more exploratory space through digital tools and form-making. More recently, physical models have become a way of bringing these ways of thinking together, an intuitive space where structure, material, form, and performance can be tested simultaneously.

I’m particularly interested in the relationship between structural efficiency, aesthetics and in how material and construction can become sources of architectural expression. I’m drawn to projects where technical decisions are not separated from the cultural and social questions architecture inevitably carries.
