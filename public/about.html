Structure, Material, Form

Architectural designer and professional engineer, trained at Columbia University.

My work explores how the experience of space, the efficiency of structure, and the character of materials shape architecture. Rooted in engineering and informed by experience in development, my process has evolved from mathematics and numerical analysis to digital exploration and model making. Physical models bring analytical thinking and intuition together, allowing me to test structure, material, and form simultaneously.
